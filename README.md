# news-image-20261124: "Tuesday 24 November 2026"

One front-page image (`entry.png`, 1024 x 1024 PNG) that sums up the news of
Tuesday 24 November 2026. A program in `main.ts` copies the image to
`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`. `tools/make_image.py` draws the page with Pillow.

## Build

    python tools/make_image.py      # writes entry.png at the repository root
    MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts

## Notes on wording

- Item 1: outlets gave different wounded counts (22, about 40, 44 and 48). The
  image says "dozens".
- Item 3: the six others killed are reported by medics and police, as Reuters
  reports them.
- Item 10: UKHSA's figure covers England, as BBC reports it. Reuters writes
  "Britain". The image says England.
- Item 11: the second source is an AFP dispatch republished by NAMPA.
- Items 12 and 1 come from the same AP article. Items 15, 16 and 17 come from
  one AP article too.
- Item 16: Al Jazeera's piece is an analysis of the tanker attacks, not a
  report of the event. It is the only second source for this item.
- Item 17: AP reports at least eight killed in three strikes in Khan Younis.
  Reuters reports at least three killed in one strike on a police post.

## Method and limits

- Reuters pages returned HTTP 401 to the browser in this environment. Reuters
  dates above come from the URL (reuters.com/...-2026-11-24/) and from
  syndicated copies with "(Reuters)" datelines dated 24 Nov. Al Jazeera items
  are dated from their URLs.
- AP and BBC pages were opened, and their timestamps and photo captions were
  read.
- Stories were found through the Wikipedia daily current-events page for
  24 Nov 2026, the Just Security "Early Edition" and Democracy Now headlines
  for that date, and DuckDuckGo result pages (including `site:` searches).
