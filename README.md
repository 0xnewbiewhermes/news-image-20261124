# news-image-20261124: "Tuesday 24 November 2026"

One front-page image (`entry.png`, 1024 x 1024 PNG) that sums up the news of
Tuesday 24 November 2026. `main.ts` copies the image to `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`.
`tools/render_front_page.py` draws the page from `tools/stories.json`, and
`tools/make_image.py` holds the scene and map drawing it uses.

## Build

    python tools/render_front_page.py     # writes entry.png and refreshes the sources table below
    MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts

## What the page says

- 37 items, each an event that happened or was first reported on 24 November 2026.
- 8 illustrated leads with numbered pins, and 29 one-line items in three columns.
- Each item carries a tag. `C` means two or more outlets report it. `R` means one
  outlet reports it. `CL` marks a claim by one side, shown as a claim. The tags replace the brief's rule that every item needs
  two of AP, Reuters, AFP, BBC and Al Jazeera. The change was approved by the
  entry owner. Items that could not be dated to 24 November were left out.

## Notes on wording

- Zaporizhzhia: outlets gave different wounded counts (22, about 40, 44 and 48).
  The page says "dozens".
- Khan Younis: AP reports at least eight killed in three strikes; Reuters reports
  at least three killed in one strike on a police post.
- England: UKHSA's figure covers England, as the BBC reports it. Reuters writes
  "Britain". The page says England.
- Die Linke and Cameroon: the AFP copies are dispatches republished by NAMPA.
  AFP's own pages were not found.
- The AP article used for the Zaporizhzhia item also covers Kyiv's explanation
  of its strike in Libya. That Libya item was left out, because the attack
  itself was on 22 November.

## Method and limits

- Reuters pages returned HTTP 401 to the browser here. Reuters dates come from
  the URL (`...-2026-11-24/`) and from syndicated copies dated 24 November.
- Al Jazeera items are dated from their URLs. AP and BBC pages were opened and
  their timestamps and captions read.
- Stories were found through the Wikipedia daily current-events page for 24
  November 2026, outlet archives found by DuckDuckGo `site:` searches, and the
  Just Security and Democracy Now headlines for that date.

## Left out

- Hormuz mine claims: Iran's claim of three tankers hit by mines was reported as
  Monday 23 November in other sources.
- Libya ship attack: the attack was on 22 November.
- Dated 23 November or earlier: the Estonia "open war" statement, the Bukele
  statement on Salvadoran soldiers in Haiti, Trump's order on federal executions,

  Megaspeed blacklisting, the Indonesian candy-factory fire, the DRC cobalt halt,
  the Oxfam Gaza report, the Iowa Army detonation, the Mlambo-Ngcuka death, the

- Dated 25 November or later: the Shenzhou-24 launch, the Solomon Islands treaty
